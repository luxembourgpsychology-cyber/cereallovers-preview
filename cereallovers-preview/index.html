    THE ROOM · BEGGEN

    Atelier

    Not a classroom.
    A working bar.

    Proper espresso machines, grinders you can actually dial in, scales, a cupping table, and enough space that everybody gets their hands on the equipment instead of watching over a shoulder.

    We train on Ascaso. Commercial grade, the machine you meet in a real café, so what you learn transfers to the one you will stand behind.

    small groups. everybody pulls shots. nobody spectates.

    Coffee Lovers Barista Academy is registered by the Government of Luxembourg as an accredited training centre.
