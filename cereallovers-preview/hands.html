Which hand writes
in the margins?

Neue Haas and PP Migra are fixed by the toolbox. This is only about the third voice: the felt tip that annotates. Every face below is free for commercial use and every one covers é, è, ü and ß, so it survives FR / EN / DE. Same words in each, so you can judge like for like.

SHANTELL SANSARTIST'S MARKER · VARIABLERUNNER UP

good food this way.

serious about coffee. questionable about everything else.

follow the pink.keep going.no clocks.your turn.

Drawn from Shantell Martin's own marker, and she is a gallery artist, not a lettering vendor, so it reads as a person who draws rather than a font imitating handwriting. Real weights, real italics, full punctuation. The one flaw: the stroke is monoline and quite round, so next to Migra's thick and thin it looks like it came from a different world. Pick it if you want more volume than Mansalva without going to Permanent Marker.

PERMANENT MARKERFAT SHARPIE · CAPS ONLYTHE LOUD OPTION

good food this way.

serious about coffee. questionable about everything else.

follow the pink.keep going.no clocks.your turn.

The loudest, and the most literal match to the fat black pen in your sketches. Two things to know before you pick it: there is no real lowercase, so every line comes out shouting, and one weight means it cannot speak quietly anywhere. It also carries a flyer association that works against the concept store framing. Say the word and I swap it in, it is one line of CSS.

MANSALVAFLAT BRUSH PENMY PICK · STATEMENTS

good food this way.

serious about coffee. questionable about everything else.

follow the pink.keep going.no clocks.your turn.

The only face here with real stroke contrast: thick on the downstroke, thin on the cross, exactly like a flat marker held at an angle. That is also what Migra does, which is why these two look like they were made by the same hand on the same afternoon. It has genuine lowercase, so it can say a quiet thing and a loud thing. Less volume than Permanent Marker, and that is the trade: it looks drawn rather than printed.

The better answer, if you want it. None of these is your designer's hand. Ask her to write about twenty of these lines once, on paper, with a real marker. Scan them and cut them to SVG the same way Migra was cut for the wordmark. The site then stops borrowing a font and starts using your handwriting, which is what the fashion references actually do. The nine above are the stand in until that exists.
