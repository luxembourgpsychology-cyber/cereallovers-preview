What it costs
to run.

Three things cost money, or might: where the site lives, how the sign-up note reaches us, and keeping the course dates fresh. Prices were checked on 6 October 2026. Vercel prices are in US dollars, before VAT.

short version: the preview is free. going live is about $20 a month.

1 · Where the site lives

The preview runs on Ivana's own Vercel account today. That is fine for showing it to us. Vercel's free plan is for personal, non-commercial use only, so the real café website needs the paid plan. It does not need a second account: the same login upgrades to Pro, ideally as a team the company pays for.

Vercel Hobby, what runs the preview now

Free. Personal, non-commercial use only. Right for a private review link, not for the live business site.

$0PER MONTH

Vercel Pro, for the live site

$20 per editor seat per month. Viewers are free. One seat is enough to run it. Commercial use allowed, email support, and cereallovers.lu points at it with one DNS change.

$20PER MONTH, 1 SEAT

Stay on Wix

Keep the current Wix plan and rebuild this design inside the Wix editor. No new bill, but the scribbles, the dates that update themselves and the handwritten note only partly survive the move.

current planNO CHANGE

2 · The “send me more info” note

The note works today at no cost. A service only changes where the message goes.

As it works now: it opens the visitor's email

Pressing “send it” opens the visitor's own email app with the note already written to hello@cereallovers.lu. They press send there. Free, nothing to set up. Some people will not press send.

$0NO ACCOUNT

Formspree, the note arrives by itself

The note goes straight to our inbox, no email app needed. Same handwritten design. Free for 50 sign-ups a month, then $15 a month. Setup is one line in the course file.

$0–15PER MONTH

Tally, a form builder

Unlimited forms and sign-ups on the free plan, straight into a Google Sheet if we want. The catch: it puts Tally's own form in place of the handwritten note. Pro, without their branding, is €20 a month.

€0–20PER MONTH

3 · Keeping the courses fresh

Free. Every course, date and line on the ticker comes from one small text file, courses.json. Change a date there and the site changes within a minute. Nothing to log into apart from GitHub.

  1. A session disappears by itself the day after it ends, so the page never shows an old date.
  2. The countdown (“next one in 6 days”) and the “updated” stamp work themselves out.
  3. Turning prices on or off is one switch in the same file.
